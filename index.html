🛡 Unblock websites

Optional – not needed for normal use. Sites that refuse to open inside the app already open in a window beside it. Only connect a proxy if you want them inside the app (needs a free Cloudflare Worker, about 2 minutes).

  1. Tap Copy Worker code.
  2. Open Cloudflare → Workers & Pages → Create → Hello World. Delete the sample code, paste, press Deploy.
  3. Copy your Worker link (like https://name.you.workers.dev) and paste it below.
Open Cloudflare ↗
